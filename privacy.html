Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026 · Reviewed at least once every 12 months

Bean Bros Brewing Co. ("Bean Bros," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through beanbrosbrewingco.com (the "Site"), how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to visitors, customers, and Subscribe & Save subscribers in the United States.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Order & account information: name, email address, phone number, and shipping/billing address, collected when you check out or manage a subscription.
  • Payment information: processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe, on Stripe-hosted checkout pages. We never see or store your full card number — see Section 3.
  • Communications: anything you send us by email, such as customer service requests.
  • Marketing sign-up: if you opt in to receive emails from us, we collect your email address for that purpose only.

Information collected automatically

Netlify and Stripe may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps for security and service operation. When configured and you opt in, Google Analytics measures visits and shopping activity; Meta Pixel and TikTok Pixel measure advertising activity. These providers may receive browser and device information, the page URL, identifiers, and product interactions. Our custom shopping events do not include your name, email, payment details, or shipping address. When you choose optional measurement at checkout, we attach permitted browser identifiers and your choices to the payment session. After Stripe confirms payment, configured platforms receive a purchase event with an opaque transaction reference, products and amounts. This can occur even if you do not return to the store. Temporary delivery records are cleared after successful delivery or within 48 hours; transaction references remain to prevent duplicate reporting.

2. How We Use Information

  • To process and fulfill orders, including recurring Subscribe & Save shipments
  • To create and manage your subscription and provide self-service billing management
  • To communicate with you about your order, shipment, or account
  • To send marketing emails, only if you've opted in, and always with an unsubscribe link
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud or abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as tax and accounting recordkeeping

4. Cookies

Optional analytics and advertising scripts load only after your consent. Use Cookie settings at the bottom of the storefront or this page to accept, reject, or change your choices. We remember choices for up to 180 days. Global Privacy Control disables advertising tracking. Withdrawing consent stops future optional tracking; it does not delete data already received by a provider. Essential account and shopping-bag storage remains available. Stripe uses its own cookies on hosted checkout and billing pages.

5. Marketing Emails

We send marketing email only to people who have asked for it. You can opt in from the mailing-list form on our site, or by ticking the optional box at checkout or when you start a Subscribe & Save plan. That box is never pre-ticked, and leaving it unticked has no effect on your order, your price, or your subscription — you will still receive the order confirmations, shipping notices, subscription acknowledgments, renewal reminders and receipts that go with what you bought, because those are service messages rather than advertising, and they cannot be unsubscribed from while you have an active order or subscription.

Every marketing email includes a working unsubscribe link and our physical business address, and we honor opt-out requests promptly, consistent with the CAN-SPAM Act. We keep a record of when and how you opted in. We will never sell or rent your email address to third parties.

6. Data Retention

We retain order and subscription records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle returns or disputes, and meet our tax and accounting obligations, after which we delete or anonymize the data.

7. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, or to unsubscribe from marketing emails, at any time by emailing hello@beanbrosbrewingco.com with the subject line "Privacy Request". Active Subscribe & Save subscriptions can be managed or canceled directly through the self-service billing portal linked from your account, and you can view and edit your name and address yourself from your account page.

How we handle a request. We acknowledge it within 10 business days and respond substantively within 45 days; if we genuinely need longer we will tell you why and may extend once by a further 45 days. To protect your data we will verify the request comes from you — normally by confirming you control the email address on the account, and by asking about a recent order if anything looks unclear. We will not charge you, and we will not treat you differently for asking. If we cannot fully honor a request we will explain which part and why.

What deletion means. We will delete your account and marketing records. We must keep certain order and payment records to meet tax, accounting and fraud-prevention obligations, and Stripe retains its own transaction records under its policies; we will tell you what has been retained and why. Deleting your account does not by itself cancel an active subscription — cancel that first, or ask us to do both.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect about you, request its deletion or correction, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address above; we will not discriminate against you for making a request.

Other State Privacy Laws

Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws (for example Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah) have similar rights to access, correct, delete, and opt out of targeted advertising or sale of personal data. The same contact above applies.

Where We Operate

Bean Bros Brewing Co. is a United States business. We sell and ship only within the United States, our website and marketing are directed only at customers in the United States, and we do not offer goods or services to, or monitor the behavior of, people in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. Your information is stored and processed in the United States by the providers listed in Section 3. If we begin selling or shipping internationally, we will update this policy before doing so.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Payment information is handled entirely by Stripe on PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure; we never receive or store full card numbers.

For the information we do hold: the Site is served only over HTTPS with HSTS; account passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes and are never stored or logged in readable form; customer and administrative sessions use signed, expiring tokens, and every endpoint that returns your orders, subscriptions or profile verifies that token before responding, so one customer's details cannot be retrieved by supplying another customer's email address; customer, order and catalog records live in a managed PostgreSQL database that is not reachable from the public internet and is backed up on a schedule; and administrative access requires a separate password and is limited to the shop owner. A browser content security policy restricts what code the Site is allowed to run.

No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you and any regulator required by law, without unreasonable delay.

10. Changes to This Policy

We review this policy at least once every 12 months and will update the "Last updated" date above whenever we make changes. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at hello@beanbrosbrewingco.com or write to us at the address below.
