# Bean Bros — informative coffee campaign research
Research checked September 6, 2026.

## Product claims
Primary brand source: https://beanbrosbrewingco.com/.netlify/functions/products

Mexican Chiapas: dark roast; Chiapas, Mexico; 1,200–1,800 m; dark chocolate, brown sugar, roasted nuts, smoky finish; smooth body and deep sweetness. The bag confirms dark roast and Chiapas origin.

Legender Brazil: Minas Gerais; milk chocolate, caramel, hazelnut, brown sugar; natural processing. Catalog says Medium-Dark but bag says Medium Roast, so omit roast level from added ad copy. Do not repeat the catalog’s generic Santos wording or add an organic certification claim from the bag.

Nicaragua: catalog calls this Fara Estate Nicaragua, with caramel, peach, hazelnut, medium roast, and Matagalpa family-estate context. Bag calls it Nicaragua and lists sweet red fruit, milk chocolate, caramel, toasted nuts. User requested Nicaraguan Reserve. Clarification requested before assigning a specific profile to that name.

## External research and limits
- https://www.ictcoffee.com/import-origins/mexican-coffee/ — Chiapas generally grows at 1,200–1,800 m and is associated with clean, balanced nut and fruit profiles. These general regional notes do not override the brand’s dark-roasted product profile.
- https://www.gob.mx/se/articulos/sabias-que-el-cafe-chiapas-tiene-denominacion-de-origen — Mexican government context on Chiapas geography, volcanic soils and suitable growing climate. Do not imply this product holds a designation certification.
- https://www.legendercoffees.com/nossa-origem?lang=en — Legender’s own account identifies Sul de Minas Gerais and smallholder partnerships. Use region context, not unverified lot-specific farm, altitude or producer payment claims.
- https://www.perccoffee.com/products/brazil — another roaster’s Legender profile supports broad chocolate/nut/sweetness context, but its exact roast, recipe and taste notes are not Bean Bros specifications.
- https://www.faracoffee.com/pages/about — Fara describes shade-grown, hand-picked Arabica from family farms in Matagalpa. Do not apply its charity, certification or roasting-location claims to Bean Bros.
- https://interamericancoffee.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Factsheet_NIC_Matagalpa-Region.pdf — northern-highland geography and climate context. Its lot-specific process, varieties and tasting notes are not automatically applicable.

## Creative approach
Three product-specific informative campaigns, each with a readable image ad, a 15-second narrated hook and a 45-second educational edit. Custom matte-black ceramic mugs carry the supplied Bean Bros logo. Mugs are visual props; no claim that they are available, free or included.
Use origin, tasting notes and an accessible explanation of the cup. Tasting-note foods are sensory comparisons, not added ingredients. Avoid unverified health, caffeine, certification, scarcity or price claims.
