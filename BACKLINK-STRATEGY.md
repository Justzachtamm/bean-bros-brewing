# Bean Bros backlink plan

Prepared September 17, 2026. This is a proposed outreach plan; no messages, directory submissions, memberships, paid placements or link purchases have been made.

## Positioning and destinations

Lead with Bean Bros as a Paramus-based online coffee brand: roast-to-order coffee, 16 oz bags, illustrated packaging, and a choice of grinds. Link general brand coverage to `https://beanbrosbrewingco.com/`; use `/shop/` for the current catalog and the actual canonical product URL for a specific bag. Do not describe Bean Bros as a walk-in café or claim a physical customer location without confirmation. Do not use unsubstantiated health benefits to promote herbs or mushroom powders.

## Outreach approach

For each prospect, record why its audience fits, the editor's published contact route, the specific story or resource offered, and the best destination URL. Start with five personalized pitches, then one follow-up after 7–10 business days. Confirm current contacts on the prospect's own website immediately before sending. Do not invent prior relationships, reviews, partnerships or press interest.

A sample or sponsorship must not require a favorable review or a ranking-passing link. Disclose gifts and sponsorships; ask publishers to use `rel="sponsored"` (or `nofollow`) where appropriate. Avoid purchased dofollow links, bulk guest-post packages, automated comments, reciprocal-link requirements and private blog networks. [Google's link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) distinguishes earned editorial links from manipulative arrangements.

## Measurement

Maintain a simple prospect sheet: prospect, relevance, contact URL, target page, contact date, follow-up date, response, placement URL, link attribute, and referral results. Review monthly. Prioritize relevant referring domains, engaged visits and orders over raw link count or third-party authority scores. Use Search Console's Links report for discovery and consented analytics for referrals; neither guarantees complete attribution. A practical first-month activity target is five qualified pitches and two confirmed listing opportunities, not a promised number of backlinks.
