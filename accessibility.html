Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Bean Bros Brewing Co. is committed to making beanbrosbrewingco.com usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnification, or keyboard-only navigation. We're targeting conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. We use these guidelines to guide ongoing improvements to the site.

Measures We've Taken

  • Descriptive alternative text on meaningful images and icon-only controls
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, product cards, and cart controls, with visible focus indicators
  • A "skip to main content" link at the top of each page for keyboard and screen-reader users
  • Motion and animation that respects your operating system's "reduce motion" setting
  • Semantic HTML headings and landmark structure on our policy pages, so screen readers can navigate them by section
  • Improved text contrast, form boundaries, and touch targets across customer pages

Known Limitations

We review customer pages with automated accessibility checks and keyboard testing. These checks do not establish complete conformance. Further testing with screen readers and people with disabilities remains part of our ongoing work.

Secure payment fields, address suggestions, and digital wallets use embedded Stripe components. Billing management may open a Stripe-hosted page. These services and their updates need continued accessibility review alongside our own pages. If any part of shopping or managing an order is difficult to use, contact us for help.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on our Site, please tell us — we want to fix it. Email hello@beanbrosbrewingco.com with the page URL and a description of the issue, and we'll respond within 5 business days.
