Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Bean Bros Brewing Co. is committed to making beanbrosbrewingco.com usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology such as screen readers, screen magnification, or keyboard-only navigation. We're targeting conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, the standard most commonly referenced under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Measures We've Taken

  • Descriptive alternative text on meaningful images and icon-only controls
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, product cards, and cart controls, with visible focus indicators on every interactive element
  • A "skip to main content" link at the top of each page for keyboard and screen-reader users
  • Motion and animation that respects your operating system's "reduce motion" setting
  • Semantic HTML headings and landmark structure on our policy pages, so screen readers can navigate them by section
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background on our policy and account pages

Known Limitations

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time fix. Parts of the storefront use custom, animation-heavy interface components; we review and improve these regularly. Checkout and subscription billing management happen on pages hosted directly by Stripe, which maintains its own accessibility conformance outside our direct control.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on our Site, please tell us — we want to fix it. Email hello@beanbrosbrewingco.com with the page URL and a description of the issue, and we'll respond within 5 business days.
